\section*{Brand Management}
Meaning of branding, role of branding, Brand equity, branding strategies, brand management

\section*{1. Meaning of Branding}
Branding is the process of creating a unique name, identity, image, and perception for a product, service, or organization in the minds of consumers. It goes beyond just a logo or tagline-it encompasses everything that shapes how people feel about and recognize a company or product.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Branding $=$ The art and science of making your offering memorable, meaningful, and different from competitors.
  \item A brand is the sum total of all perceptions, experiences, and associations that a customer has with a company or product.
\end{itemize}

As Amazon's Jeff Bezos famously said:\\
"Your brand is what people say about you when you're not in the room."

\section*{2. Role of Branding}
Branding plays several critical roles in business success:\\
i) Identification \& Differentiation

Helps customers quickly recognize and distinguish your product in a crowded market.\\
ii) Trust \& Credibility

Strong brands reduce perceived risk and build customer confidence.\\
iii) Customer Loyalty

Emotional connection leads to repeat purchases and advocacy.\\
iv) Premium Pricing Power

Strong brands can charge more than generic competitors (e.g., Apple vs. generic smartphones).\\
v) Business Value

A strong brand is a valuable intangible asset (e.g., Coca-Cola's brand value is worth billions).\\
vi) Marketing Efficiency

Well-known brands spend less on customer acquisition over time.

\section*{3. Brand Equity}
Brand equity refers to the added value a brand name gives to a product or service. It is the measurable "goodwill" or premium that a brand commands.

\subsection*{Components of Brand Equity (David Aaker Model):}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Brand Awareness - How many people know the brand?
  \item Brand Associations - What qualities or emotions come to mind?
  \item Perceived Quality - Customers' opinion about the product's superiority.
  \item Brand Loyalty - Degree to which customers repeatedly choose the brand.
  \item Proprietary Assets - Patents, trademarks, channel relationships.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Keller's Customer-Based Brand Equity (CBBE) Model}
Builds brand equity in a pyramid:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Brand Identity (Salience)
  \item Brand Meaning (Performance \& Imagery)
  \item Brand Response (Judgments \& Feelings)
  \item Brand Resonance (Loyalty, Attachment, Community, Engagement)
\end{itemize}

High brand equity → Higher customer preference, resilience to crises, and financial valuation.

\section*{4. Branding Strategies}
Companies use different strategies depending on their goals and market position:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
Strategy & Description & Example \\
\hline
Corporate Branding & Uses company name across all products & Apple, Google, Virgin \\
\hline
Product Branding & Individual brand names for each product & Procter \& Gamble (Pampers, Tide, Gillette \\
\hline
Family/Umbrella Branding & One brand name for a range of products & Nestlé Samsung \\
\hline
Individual Branding & Each product has a distinct identity & Unilever (Dove, Axe, Lipton) \\
\hline
Co-Branding & Two brands collaborate on a product & Nike + Apple (Nike+) \\
\hline
Private Labeling & Retailer creates its own brand & Walmart's Great Value \\
\hline
Brand Extension & Using an existing brand name on a new product category & Virgin (Airlines → Music → Mobile) \\
\hline
Line Extension & New variants under the same brand (flavors, sizes, etc.) & Coca-Cola Zero, Diet Coke \\
\hline
Brand Repositioning & Changing the brand's image or target market & Old Spice (from old to cool) \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section*{5. Brand Management}
Brand management is the ongoing process of building, maintaining, measuring, and protecting brand equity throughout the brand's lifecycle.

\subsection*{Key elements in Brand Management:}
i) Brand Positioning- Defining how the brand is different and better.\\
ii) Brand Identity Development - Creating visual (logo, colors) and verbal (tone, tagline) elements.\\
iii) Brand Communication - Advertising, PR, social media, packaging.\\
iv) Brand Experience - Ensuring every customer touchpoint reflects the brand promise.\\
v) Brand Tracking - Measuring awareness, perception, and equity using surveys, NPS, etc.\\
vi) Brand Protection - Trademark registration, fighting counterfeits.\\
vii) Brand Portfolio Management - Deciding which brands to invest in, maintain, or retire.\\
viii) Crisis Management - Protecting brand reputation during negative events.

\subsection*{Brand Management Cycle:}
Research → Strategy → Identity → Launch → Communication → Experience → Measurement → Adjustment → Protection

A strong brand is one of the most valuable and sustainable competitive advantages a company can have.\\
