Which of the following best describes a fuel?
A. A substance used to cool engines
B. A combustible substance that releases energy during combustion
C. A material that absorbs heat from the surroundings
D. A substance used only for electricity generation
ANSWER: B

Which of the following fuels is classified as a solid fuel?
A. Natural gas
B. Diesel
C. Coal
D. LPG
ANSWER: C

Which of the following statements about Lower Calorific Value (LCV) is correct?
A. It includes the latent heat of vaporization of water
B. It excludes the latent heat of vaporization of water
C. It is always greater than HCV
D. It is used only for gaseous fuels
ANSWER: B

A coal sample has the following proximate analysis: Moisture = 6%, Volatile matter = 28%, Ash = 14%. What is the fixed carbon percentage?
A. 48%
B. 50%
C. 52%
D. 54%
ANSWER: C

Which component of ultimate analysis is determined by difference?
A. Carbon
B. Hydrogen
C. Sulfur
D. Oxygen
ANSWER: D

Which element in fuel contributes most to the heating value?
A. Nitrogen
B. Carbon
C. Ash
D. Moisture
ANSWER: B

How much oxygen is required for complete combustion of 1 kg of hydrogen?
A. 2 kg
B. 4 kg
C. 8 kg
D. 16 kg
ANSWER: C

A combustion system operates with AFR_actual greater than AFR_stoich. This condition is known as:
A. Rich mixture
B. Lean mixture
C. Stoichiometric mixture
D. Saturated mixture
ANSWER: B

Which of the following gases is a major greenhouse gas produced during combustion?
A. CO2
B. O2
C. N2
D. H2
ANSWER: A

Why is excess air supplied in practical combustion systems?
A. To reduce combustion temperature only
B. To ensure complete combustion of fuel
C. To increase ash production
D. To reduce oxygen in the exhaust
ANSWER: B
