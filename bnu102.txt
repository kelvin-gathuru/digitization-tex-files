Which of the following segments is the longest part of the large intestine?
A. Cecum
B. Ascending colon
C. Transverse colon
D. Sigmoid colon
ANSWER: D

Which of the following structures is located in the left lower quadrant of the abdomen?
A. Appendix
B. Ascending colon
C. Descending colon
D. Cecum
ANSWER: C

The appendix is attached to which part of the large intestine?
A. Cecum
B. Ascending colon
C. Transverse colon
D. Rectum
ANSWER: A

The primary function of the colon is to:
A. Absorb nutrients
B. Store bile
C. Absorb water and electrolytes
