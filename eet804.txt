Which of the following is a key advantage of natural fibers in construction?
A. High cost of production
B. Non-biodegradability
C. Renewable and eco-friendly nature
D. High energy consumption
ANSWER: C

Natural fibers contribute to sustainable development primarily by:
A. Increasing carbon emissions
B. Reducing environmental impact
C. Replacing all construction materials
D. Eliminating the need for cement
ANSWER: B

Which of the following is NOT a commonly used natural fiber in construction?
A. Jute
B. Sisal
C. Hemp
D. Nylon
ANSWER: D

What is a major limitation of natural fibers in construction materials?
A. High density
B. Moisture sensitivity
C. Excessive strength
D. Non-availability
ANSWER: B

Natural fiber-reinforced materials improve construction performance by:
A. Increasing brittleness
B. Enhancing crack resistance
C. Reducing flexibility
D. Eliminating durability concerns
ANSWER: B

Which of the following are natural fibers used in construction?
A. Jute
B. Sisal
C. Polyester
D. Hemp
ANSWER: ABD

Natural fibers support sustainability through:
A. Biodegradability
B. Low carbon footprint
C. High energy demand
D. Renewability
ANSWER: ABD

Challenges associated with natural fibers include:
A. Moisture absorption
B. Variability in properties
C. Corrosion resistance
D. Biological degradation
ANSWER: ABD
